📚 Chapter Summary: Your schema is the collection of everything you have learned so far. Reading, watching movies, listening to songs, and new experiences all add to it — and connecting new knowledge to old knowledge helps you understand a text better.

Building SchemaReading stories, watching movies, listening to songs, and new experiences all add to it

QUESTIONS TO ASK WHILE READING Did this happen to me before? What did I do? Did I do something similar to or different from the character?

What HappenedAngie got sick because she did not wash her hands before eating; the doctor said she was infected by microbes
📚 Chapter Summary: Signal words of time order — first, next, then, after that, and finally — introduce the steps in a process or the sequence of events in a story. Recognizing them helps you understand a text's structure.
🔢 Signal Words of Time Order
FirstIntroduces the very first step
NextIntroduces the step after the first
ThenIntroduces a step in the middle of the sequence
After ThatIntroduces a step following "then"
FinallyIntroduces the last step
MAYA'S SANDWICH — CORRECT ORDER 1. First, Maya prepared two slices of bread. → 2. Next, she spread peanut butter on one slice. → 3. Then, she spread the strawberry jam on another slice. → 4. After that, she put the two slices of bread together. → 5. Finally, she ate it.
📚 Chapter Summary: Fantasy stories have elements that cannot happen in real life. Reality stories show situations that may happen in real life, though imagined. A fantasy story can still include realistic details — it's the overall theme that decides the type.
FantasyReality
Has elements that cannot happen in real life; exercises creativity and imaginationShows situations that may happen in real life, although imagined
Example: Harry Potter (wizards, witches, spells)Example: Sherlock Holmes, Heidi
REMEMBER Even fantasy stories can include real-life situations (like going to school or riding a train) — it's the overall magical theme that makes a story fantasy, not every single detail in it.
✅ True or False — Key Reminders
FALSEConnecting new and old knowledge makes a text harder to understand → it actually makes it easier and more relatable.
FALSE"Finally" introduces the very first step → "First" does; "Finally" introduces the last step.
FALSEFantasy stories have elements that can happen in real life → fantasy has elements that CANNOT happen in real life.
FALSEFantasy stories can never contain any elements of reality → they can; some parts may reflect real life even in a fantasy story.
FALSEHeidi is a fantasy story about wizards → Heidi is a reality-based story about a girl growing up in the mountains.
